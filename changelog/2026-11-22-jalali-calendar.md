---
title: 'Jalali calendar'
type: feature
components: [calendar, date-picker, date-range-picker, filter-box, data-filter]
---

`calendar="jalali"` shows the Persian calendar (Persian months and digits, weeks starting on Saturday, right to left) in Calendar, Date Picker, Date Range Picker, Filter Box and Data Filter. Values stay plain `Date`s, so forms keep sending Gregorian dates.
